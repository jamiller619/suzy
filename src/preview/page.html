Small details, better days.

A good workspace gives your ideas room to grow.

Start with a clear title and a little breathing room. A comfortable line length helps readers move through longer passages without losing their place.

Monospace / code and shortcuts

A place for precise details.

Browser fonts are used by default. Configure body or mono font families to override them.

.article {
  font-size: var(--suzy-font-size-3);
  line-height: var(--suzy-line-height-relaxed);
}

Press Ctrl + S to save your changes.
